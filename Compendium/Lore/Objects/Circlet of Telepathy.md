### Description
While wearing this circlet, you can use an action to cast the **detect thoughts** spell (save DC 13) from it. As long as you maintain concentration on the spell, you can use a bonus action to send a telepathic message to a creature you are focused on. It can reply--using a bonus action to do so--while your focus on it continues.

While focusing on a creature with **detect thoughts**, you can use an action to cast the **suggestion** spell (save DC 13) from the helm on that creature. Once used, the **suggestion** property can't be used again until the next dawn.
